Privacy Policy

App: Play Publish: App Wizard (io.github.socketrocketdocket.playwizard)
Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

Play Publish: App Wizard is designed to work completely offline and protect your privacy. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All checklist progress, personalization quiz answers, step notes, store listing drafts, and closed-testing tester lists you enter are stored locally on your own device using your browser or app's local storage (localStorage).

  • This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export a Markdown, PDF, or JSON backup file.
  • When you use the offline Asset Validator, image files you pick are inspected locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • You can delete all stored data at any time using the Reset Current App button in the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings.

3. Data Collection & Third-Party Services

In the current release (v1.0), this app does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs, and collects no personal or device data. External links inside the guide (such as Google Play Console and Android Developer documentation) open in your browser and are governed by their own privacy policies.

If future versions introduce optional advertising or in-app purchases via Google Play Billing, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated prior to release.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is a developer productivity tool intended for app creators aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please reach out via our GitHub repository:
